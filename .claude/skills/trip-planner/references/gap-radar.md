# Gap radar — topics planners forget

These are topics a real traveler had to raise themselves during a planning session because the planner didn't. Cover each proactively; mark "n/a" when it doesn't apply.

## Logistics
- **Airport choice by total cost**: fare + bag fees + ground transport + time, not fare alone.
- **Bag allowance per person** on each airline and fare type (carry-on vs personal item, sizes, gate checks); spare capacity for shopping.
- **Rental car details**: best pickup branch (airport vs city), one-way fees, drop-off time vs flight, early-return fees, skip-the-counter programs, additional/spouse driver, toll plan, fuel policy, deposit hold, photos at pickup.
- **Rental protection**: credit-card coverage (primary vs secondary, enrollment, flat fee per rental, not a monthly charge), personal policy (collision, comprehensive, deductible, liability limits), supplemental liability, roadside assistance (what's included vs add-on).
- **Card perks**: rental status upgrades, dining multipliers, trip delay coverage — and the requirement to pay with that card.
- **Travel insurance**: what's covered (only bookings made through the seller?), covered reasons, refund window, whether existing cards already cover it.
- **Parking**: hotel parking cost, city garages and their daily max, street rules, towing risk, no-parking hotels.
- **Day trip vs overnight**: total drive hours, night driving, and that hotels elsewhere may cost the same.

## Lodging
- **Mandatory fees**: resort/destination fees and what they include (use the perks), deposits/holds, tax on fees.
- **Refund deadlines**: free cancellation cut-offs relative to today.
- **Room type**: bed configuration vs size; accessible rooms (book standard rooms when possible).
- **Check-in/out times** vs arrival/departure; bag hold.
- **Weekend/holiday price spikes** (e.g. Friday nights at hot spots) and realistic budget alternatives; where to stay to walk everything.

## Food and drink
- **Restaurants that closed** — verify before recommending.
- **Reservation mechanics**: release windows, bar vs dining room, patio vs indoor (weather), cancellation fees, timing relative to other activities.
- **Seasonal specialties** (e.g. stone crab season) and famous drinks per bar.
- **Breakfast variety** beyond sandwiches.
- **Celebrity/famous restaurants**: fit with diet (signature dishes may conflict).

## Activities
- **Overview tours** (bus/boat): is it worth it, how passes and vouchers work, how to cover the whole loop.
- **Tour options**: many operators — pick by rating, group size, pickup, departure time.
- **Closed days** of museums/attractions.
- **Event traffic** near venues on the travel dates.
- **Shopping** (outlets) and luggage implications.
- **Famous attractions the traveler asks about** (zoo, etc.): fit vs time vs distance.

## Plan quality
- **Wake-up preference** respected; no early starts without reason.
- **Packed vs relaxed**: rest blocks; don't over-fill after the traveler adds requests.
- **Maps**: a map-app link on every place; route order equals pin order; walking routes for walkable clusters.
- **Theme and format** the traveler wants; phone readability.
- **Plain-text export** for pasting into other assistants or apps.
- **Privacy**: personal data off the shareable page; don't publish itineraries (dates away from home) to public places.

## Also consider (often forgotten entirely)
- Apps to install (rideshare, rental, airline, tours, reservations, parking, transit).
- Mobile check-in windows and boarding passes.
- What to wear (dress codes at nicer restaurants/bars; beach vs evening).
- Sunscreen rules, bug spray, rip currents/flags, heat.
- Cash needs (small vendors, card surcharges), tipping norms and auto-gratuity.
- Emergency info: rental roadside number, nearest urgent care/pharmacy, hotel phone.
- Day-before and morning-of checklists.
