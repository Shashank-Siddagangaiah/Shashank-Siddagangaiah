# Intake question bank

Ask in rounds of 3–4. Skip anything already answered. Offer a recommended option first where there's a sensible default.

## Round 1 — Trip shape
- Which dates are you free? Fixed dates or flexible window? (If flexible, check weather/events/prices before recommending days.)
- Who's traveling (ages, couple/family/friends)? Anyone with mobility or accessibility needs?
- Where are you flying/driving from? Which airports are acceptable (main vs secondary, and the ground-transport cost from each)?
- Total budget, and does it include flights?

## Round 2 — Travel style
- Pace: packed / balanced / relaxed? How many rest breaks?
- What time do you like to start the day? Any days that must start early?
- Interests (rank): culture & history, beaches, nature & wildlife, food, nightlife, shopping, photography, sports, art.
- Must-dos and must-avoids. Any sources you've collected (videos, articles, a friend's plan)? Please paste or upload them.
- Do you want one "overview" experience (bus tour, boat tour, walking tour)? Why — orientation, photos, rest?

## Round 3 — Lodging
- One base or split stays? Where do you want to splurge (rest nights) vs save (sleep-only nights)?
- Priorities: walkability, quiet, pool/beach, breakfast included, parking, adults-only, room size, bed type.
- Max nightly price including resort/destination fees.

## Round 4 — Getting there and around
- Flights: preferred airlines/times, fare type, bag allowance per person (carry-on vs personal item sizes), seat selection.
- Car: none / some days / whole trip? Driver ages, licenses (domestic/international), additional drivers.
- Tolerance for parking hassle and cost.
- Day trips: worth a full day or overnight? Willing to drive at night?

## Round 5 — Money, cards and protection
- Which credit card will you pay with? (Check its travel perks: rental car coverage — primary/secondary, trip delay/cancellation, lounge/status perks, dining multipliers.)
- Do you have personal auto insurance? Collision + comprehensive? Liability limits? Deductible?
- Do you want travel insurance? What's non-refundable so far?
- Loyalty programs (airline, hotel, car rental) to use or link.

## Round 6 — Food and drink
- Diet per traveler (allergies, vegetarian/vegan, eggs/seafood OK?, no beef/pork, halal/kosher).
- Spice tolerance and flavor preferences ("not bland", "medium spicy").
- Cuisines you love; must-try local dishes; any famous restaurants you've heard of.
- Breakfast style (big American, local, light/healthy, café).
- Drinks: alcohol yes/no, cocktails vs beer vs wine, mocktails; interest in a bar hop?
- Budget per meal; any splurge meal?

## Round 7 — Extras
- Shopping (outlets, local markets, souvenirs)? Space in luggage for purchases?
- Special occasions (birthday, anniversary) to plan around?
- Photography spots, sunrise/sunset priorities.
- Preferred format for the plan (page, PDF, plain text, calendar) and visual theme (e.g. white background).
