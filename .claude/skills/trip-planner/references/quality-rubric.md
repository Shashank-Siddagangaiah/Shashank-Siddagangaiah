# Quality rubric (score out of 10)

Score each criterion 0–1, sum, and report the total with one line per criterion that lost points. Use it on a finished plan, and on this skill itself when improving it.

| # | Criterion | 1 point when… |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake completeness | Every section of the question bank was covered or explicitly not needed; no preference misread |
| 2 | Verification | Opening days, closures, ticket rules, weather and events were checked, with sources and dates |
| 3 | Feasibility | No timing collisions; buffers for flights, vouchers, reservations; drop-offs before departures |
| 4 | Flow | Each day moves area by area with no backtracking; wake-up preference and rest blocks respected |
| 5 | Food & drink | 2–3 options per meal with dishes and diet tags; must-try list; bars with famous drinks |
| 6 | Logistics & money | Transport compared by total cost; fees, parking, insurance and card perks covered; budget recomputed |
| 7 | Bookings loop | Tracker, to-do list, budget and days agree; confirmations processed with mismatches flagged |
| 8 | Maps & links | A map link on every place; route order equals pin order; works on a phone |
| 9 | Proactivity | Gap-radar topics raised before the traveler had to ask |
| 10 | Clarity & privacy | Short, decisive, readable on a phone; no personal data on the shareable page; plain-text export offered |
